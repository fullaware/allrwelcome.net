Your Coping Toolkit 🛠️

Evidence-based skills explained in ways that make sense. Pick what works for you.

🗣️ DEAR MAN Skill

D.E.A.R. M.A.N. — How to get what you need while respecting yourself and others.

Use this when you need to stand up for yourself or ask someone for something important.

  • Describe — Say the facts: "The homework is due tomorrow"
  • Express — Share your feelings: "I feel confused about this"
  • Assert — Ask clearly: "Can you explain it again?"
  • Reinforce — Explain why it helps them: "It would help me get a better grade"
  • Mindful — Stay focused, don't get distracted
  • Appear Confident — Use your body language
  • Negotiate — Be ready to find a compromise

⚡ TIP Skill

T.I.P. — Quick ways to calm your body during intense emotions.

When feelings are too big, use your body to help calm your mind.

  • Temperature — Hold something cold (ice cube, cold water on face)
  • Intense Exercise — Do burst of activity for a few minutes
  • Paced Breathing — Breathe slower than usual
  • Paired Muscle Relaxation — Tense and relax muscles while breathing

🛑 STOP Skill

S.T.O.P. — Pause before reacting when things feel overwhelming.

  • Stop — Freeze. Don't move or speak yet.
  • Take a breath — Breathe slowly and deeply.
  • Observe — What's happening inside you and around you?
  • Proceed — Do something mindful and helpful.

🌸 GENTLE Skills

Be kind to yourself during hard times.

  • Don't judge yourself harshly
  • Help in small ways you can manage
  • Express feelings with loving kindness
  • One thing at a time
  • Explore what your emotions want to teach you
  • Be curious, not sure you're right
  • Empathize with yourself first
  • Share the load with others
  • Walk in each other's shoes
  • Leave room for forgiving yourself

🫁 Box Breathing Exercise

Breathe with the circle. It helps calm your body and mind when you feel overwhelmed.

Breathe With Me

Follow the circle...

How it works: Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, breathe out for 4, hold for 4. Repeat!

🌍 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Bring yourself to the present moment using your senses.

  • 5 things you can SEE — Look around and name 5 things
  • 4 things you can TOUCH — Feel 4 things around you
  • 3 things you can HEAR — Listen for 3 sounds
  • 2 things you can SMELL — Notice 2 smells
  • 1 thing you can TASTE — Focus on 1 taste

🧊 Safe Ice T.I.P.

When emotions are extremely intense, use cold to quickly calm your body's stress response.

  • Hold an ice cube in your hand
  • Splash cold water on your face
  • Put cold water on your wrists
  • Remember: This only works while you're doing it!

💆 Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Tense and relax each muscle group, starting from your toes and working up to your head.

  • Start at your toes — scrunch them tight for 5 seconds, then relax
  • Move to your calves — tense, hold, release
  • Work up through your legs, stomach, arms, shoulders, and face
  • Notice the difference between tense and relaxed

🌈 Safe Place Visualization

Imagine your safest, most comfortable place in detail.

  • Close your eyes and picture your safe place
  • What do you see? (colors, lights, people)
  • What do you hear? (sounds, music, voices)
  • What do you feel? (temperature, textures)
  • What do you smell? (food, flowers, fresh air)
  • Visit this place anytime you need comfort

🎯 Comfort Kit

Build your own list of things that help you feel better.

  • People I can call for support
  • Activities that make me feel calm or happy
  • Things I can do to feel safe in my body
  • Internet sites and apps that help
  • Things I can see, hear, smell, taste, and touch for comfort
